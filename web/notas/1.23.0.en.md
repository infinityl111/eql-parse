## On opening, it will re-read your log once

**It takes about three quarters of a minute** — measured: 42 seconds over a log
of 1.5 million lines — and you can keep using the app meanwhile. A notice
explains it and nothing starts until you accept.

It is needed because the saved fights didn't know **which visit to the zone**
they happened in, and that fact isn't anywhere but in the log. Without it, the
respawn ceiling could count gaps between two different entries — where the enemy
didn't come back, it was born with the new copy. No combat figure changes.

## The timer panel was frozen

It painted once when it opened and stayed still: **the countdown didn't move**,
a death didn't restart it, and the × on a row removed the timer but not the row.
It's alive now.

If you wondered why the number never changed, it wasn't you.

## A candidate's card, on hover

Hover any row of the **{{cro.add}}** list and its card comes up: where, how many
times you have killed it there, its ceiling if it has one, and the level you
have seen when considering it — as a **range**, with how many considers hold it
up. Never as a single figure: the same enemy, in the same zone and difficulty,
comes up with different levels.

And its loot in two separate blocks: **what you have looted**, which is
measured, and what the wiki says, which we don't have yet and is stated instead
of left blank.

## Size and transparency, inside the overlay itself

The size slider sits next to the transparency one, in the panel and not in
Settings. Each overlay remembers its own.

## Ceilings that bounded nothing

A respawn ceiling only holds if both deaths are the same creature. The ones
resting on a gap between two different visits are gone, and some change a lot:
one said «no more than 280 minutes» and its real ceiling is nine and a half.
