## Everything you have ever killed, in one list

The **{{cro.add}}** tab is no longer empty: it brings the enemies from your
history, with their zone and difficulty already filled in. Search by name or by
zone, press **{{cro.candPoner}}** and the timer is born complete. Nothing to
type, and no need to have the creature in front of you.

Each one says how many times it has fallen there and when the last one was, and
the ones you already watch come marked. The most recent goes on top: last
night's zone is the first without looking for it.

## Timers that couldn't find their deaths

In zones with a difficulty, a timer could stay on «{{cro.esperando}}» forever
even if you had killed that enemy there many times — and then it never
restarted. It finds them now: they count again, with their observations and
their ceiling.

## A fourth state: the one that estimates nothing

A timer can know when the enemy died and not know how long it takes to come
back. That used to stay mute, or say «{{cro.disponible}}», which is claiming
something with nothing to claim it from. Now it says the only thing that can be
said, and it is a fact: how long it has been since it died.

## The panel, sorted by what is about to happen

On top, what the clock says — the ones already due and the ones counting down;
below, the ones whose time we don't know. A timer with no estimate is not more
urgent than one with twenty seconds left, and it used to come first.

## What you type doesn't erase itself

The field for setting the time by hand went back to its previous value if the
timer repainted while you were typing. Not any more: what you have touched
stays.
