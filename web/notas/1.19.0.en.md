## You can type in Respawns now

The name field would not let you type: you pressed a key and it vanished. That
is fixed, and with it **the fight list search box**, which lost letters for the
same reason. It was one fault in two different places.

## Set a timer straight from a fight

A **"{{cro.escBoton}}"** button in Scene, with a fight open. It unfolds that
fight's enemies and you tick the ones you want to watch.

It is a better route than typing the name by hand, and not for convenience: from
there come **the name exactly as the log wrote it, the zone, the difficulty, and
the exact time that enemy died in that fight**. Zone and difficulty are part of
what tells one timer from another, and typing the name by hand there was no way
to set them.

The list hides nothing and says what it cannot do:

- an enemy that **never died** still shows, but cannot be ticked: there is
  nothing to count from;
- **enemy pets** do not show, because they do not come back on a timer;
- the ones that **already have a timer** are marked, so you don't open the same
  one twice;
- and if the same name **died twice in the same fight**, it tells you they are
  two different creatures and counts from the last one.
