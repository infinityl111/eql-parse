## Respawns looks different now

The section has been rebuilt from scratch. It used to be a grid of cards; now it
is a **list of collapsible rows**: each timer takes one line, and you open the
one you care about. With four or five open you no longer have to scroll.

And with the row closed you see what matters:

- the enemy's **name**,
- the **three sources** — «{{pz.src.tuyo}}», «{{pz.src.zona}}» and
  «{{pz.src.visto}}» — all three, always, without unfolding anything;
- and the **countdown**, with a label underneath saying which of the three it
  comes from.

The one driving the number is **filled in**; the other two are outlined. That is
the one thing on this screen that is not up for negotiation: a number without
saying where it comes from is half a fact.

## And four controls at the top that were not there

- a **search box**, always visible;
- **«{{cro.agrZona}}»** or **«{{cro.agrNada}}»**, for when your timers are in
  different places;
- two filters, **«{{cro.filContando}}»** and **«{{cro.filDisponible}}»**, each
  **with its own count inside**: the number tells you whether pressing it is
  worth it;
- and **«{{pz.comoda}}» / «{{pz.densa}}»**, which tightens the rows.

The **«{{pz.leyenda}}»** legend explains the three sources once, folds away, and
remembers how you left it.

## What has not changed

Not one figure. What we have observed still isn't shown as if it were a period:
**with few observations no number is given**, and neither is one for a name with
several individuals behind it, because we don't know which one came back. What
the card says in that case is how many observations it has and why it is keeping
quiet.

## And two things that arrived in 1.19.1

In case you missed them, because they are not from this version:

- **you can type in the name field** — it used to swallow what you typed;
- and from a fight, the **«{{cro.escBoton}}»** button in Scene sets timers on
  that fight's enemies, with their zone, their difficulty and the exact time of
  death.
