## The whole application in one sidebar, and not a single figure has moved

The three tabs at the top —Combat, Alerts and Encyclopedia— are gone. In their
place there is a bar on the left with **fourteen sections**, grouped not by kind
of data but by **scope**: what each number is talking about.

**{{sec.g.pelea}}** — {{sec.escena}} · {{sec.habilidad}} · {{loot.title}} ·
{{log.title}} · {{an.tab}}

**{{sec.g.historico}}** — {{sec.resumen}} · {{enc.enemigos}} · {{enc.botin}} ·
{{sec.progreso}} · {{enc.zonas}} · {{sec.hechizos}} · {{sec.muertes}}

**{{sec.g.ajustes}}** — {{tab.alerts}} · {{sec.preferencias}}

## Why by scope

{{loot.title}} inside **{{sec.g.pelea}}** and {{loot.title}} inside
**{{sec.g.historico}}** are two different questions, and until now you reached
both by routes that never said which was which. The first thing you need to know
about a number is what it is talking about; now the group it lives in says so
before you read it.

And the fight list shows up **only where it means something**: in the five
{{sec.g.pelea}} sections, which are the ones that change when you pick another
fight. In the history ones it is not there, and the section takes the full width.

## Not a single number changed, and that is the promise

**This version computes nothing new and computes nothing differently.** It is a
move: what was there has been carried somewhere else on screen, whole.

- **It does not ask you to rebuild.** Your history stays exactly as it is, and
  the first launch takes no longer than any other.
- **The same fight shows the same figures** as in 1.15.0. If any one of them
  were different that would not be an improvement: it would be a bug, and that
  is why it was checked section by section.
- **There are no new features.** The ones here are the usual ones, in their
  place.

It is said this plainly because it is what was promised at the start, not an
apology for what is missing.

## Two things you do notice

**Timers no longer switch off when you change screen.** They used to live inside
Combat: you went to look something up in the bestiary and the countdown you were
watching vanished while it was still running. They belong to the frame now and
show in every section.

**The replay opens inside {{sec.escena}}**, without taking you out of the fight:
the header chart steps aside so the same thing is not drawn twice, and the scene
mounts right there, with the fight list still beside it.

**And the note on the three speeds now gives the calculation.** It no longer
says what the first one compares against: it says total damage ÷ fight duration,
which is what you needed to know.
