## Respawn timers

A new section. You open a timer for whichever enemy you want to watch and it
counts down on its own; you close it when you no longer care. **No death starts
one by itself** — with a hundred mobs going down, that would be screen noise and
help nobody.

The list is sorted by **whichever comes back first**, with the ones already due
at the very top. And each card says which zone and which difficulty it belongs
to, because the same mob in two places does not come back at the same rate.

## Where the time comes from, always said

On every card you see **the three sources, always all three**:

- **yours**, if you type it — it overrides everything else;
- **the wiki's**, with the page it came from, so you can go and check it;
- **what we are seeing ourselves**, which for now only accumulates.

If the wiki's number and what we have seen do not agree, **we say so and leave it
there**. We do not choose for you.

The wiki times are collected zone by zone, from each zone's own page. Four zones
do not declare one, and there the card says so rather than making a number up.

## The warnings it carries

If an enemy sits at zero for a long time, it warns you: it may already be there
and you missed it, or we may not be recognising its death line. Those are two
different things and both look the same on screen, so both get named.

And if there are several of that name in the zone, it tells you **when you open
it**, which is when it is useful: the timer will restart when any of them dies,
not the one you are watching.

## Your side stays your side, even when you swap pets

Someone could show up under **«Unidentified»** and count towards your side's dps
at the same time. It mostly happened with **your own pets**: summoning a new one
made the previous one stop being recognised, and saved fights kept that idea
forever.

Now identity is **worked out again every time you look**, using everything the
program has learnt since. Fights saved weeks ago get labelled correctly with no
rebuild — **248 fights in the history** change. And anyone genuinely unknown
still shows as unknown, which is what needs saying.

## Zones are no longer split by their difficulty

The difficulty number EQL appends to a zone name was being counted as **a
different zone**. So the encyclopedia showed the same zone several times and the
boss census came up short.

Now they are joined: a zone is a zone, and its difficulty is a column. The
encyclopedia and the census add up, and each entry carries every difficulty in
one place.
