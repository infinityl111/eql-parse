## What you destroy is counted now

When you destroy an item it stops vanishing: it shows in its own column, next to
what you have looted. **It is not subtracted from your loot** — they are two
different facts, and subtracting them would give a number that answers neither
question.

## A missing rung when you consider

There was one `/consider` answer the app read and threw away, so that creature
was left without a level. Not any more.

## Dragon Hoard loot, in its place

What comes out of the Dragon Hoard now arrives marked for what it is, and stops
mixing in with ordinary loot.
