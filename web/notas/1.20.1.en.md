## Timers now state a ceiling, and stop guessing

Until now, a timer that reached zero stayed at zero and said nothing more. Now
it says two new things, and both are **facts measured on your own log**:

**A ceiling**, with the number of gaps holding it up:

> no more than 10:35 — 6 observed gaps

That is not «its period is 10:35». It is **it has never taken longer than
that**, which is a different and far safer claim: it comes from subtracting two
of its deaths, and between those two deaths fits the period *plus* however long
you took to come back. So the real period is that or less. Never more.

That is why the gap count is always beside it: a ceiling resting on **a single
gap** is just as true and far weaker than one resting on six.

**And whether it has been seen.** Any line in the log that names the creature
proves it is there: a hit, a miss, a spell. No need to wait until you kill it.

> seen 3s ago, in a combat line → **it's there**
>
> not seen for 12:00, and its ceiling is 10:35 → **it should be there, and you
> haven't seen it**

## And the warning that guessed is gone

Where it used to say *«it has been at zero for 3 periods, it may already be
there and you missed it»* there is no longer a guess: there is the fact. Either
we named it X ago, or we have not named it since its last death.

## The «set a timer» panel was unreadable in dark

The **«{{cro.escBoton}}»** button in Scene opened a panel with a cream
background and light text on top. It was written with hardcoded colours, back
when light was the default theme. It now uses the theme's own, and reads in
both.
