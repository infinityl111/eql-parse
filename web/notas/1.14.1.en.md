## Alerts now say where they come from

If you opened the alerts tab and saw "Lady Vox" in five places you had not
written yourself, the reasonable conclusion was that the phrases came from a
wiki. They did not: that trigger **matches 33 times in your own log**, with six
of her spells. What was missing was not the rule — it was saying where it came
from.

Every trigger now carries two labels:

- **where it comes from** — "factory template", which has **not** been checked
  against your log, or "written by you"
- **what it has done** — "seen 33 times in your log", and if it has not matched
  once, it says so

The second one is what turns a promise into a fact: a pattern copied with one
word wrong never matches, and until now it looked exactly like one that fires
every day.

And it is counted **against your whole log**, not from the moment you open the
app: on startup the re-read counts without speaking. If you change logs, the
counter says it is talking about a different one and starts from zero.

The warning that boss templates have to be checked against your own log had been
written in the program since day one and appeared on no screen. Now it shows,
along with each template's note.

**The boss template no longer ships a timer.** It carried twelve seconds that
nobody had ever timed: the log never writes an enemy spell's recast time on any
line, so that number could not be measured by reading. You put it in — you are
the one who can take it with a stopwatch.

## The kills missing from the bestiary

EverQuest writes an enemy's name capitalised when it opens a sentence — "A shin
ghoul knight has been slain" — and lowercase mid-sentence — "You slash a shin
ghoul knight". The program joined both forms when counting combatants, but not
when counting deaths.

Measured on a real history: **of 4,348 kills, 25 never reached the enemy's
dossier**, and 39 estimated-health samples went with them. `orc legionnaire`
showed fourteen fewer deaths than it had, and its health estimated on half the
samples. Nine rows in an enemy's fight list said "survived" about a mob that had
died.

Nothing visibly broke, which is why it had been there forever: **a lower number
does not look any different from a correct one.**

It is fixed on reading, so your history comes out right without rebuilding
anything.

## An enemy that heals itself does not have more health

An enemy healer repairs part of the damage you do, and that is not its health:
it is damage undone. The health estimate already subtracted it — measured:
929,772 points of enemy healing, 5 % of the damage you deal them — but the rule
that decides **whether an enemy is a boss** used the figure without subtracting.

Fixed. In your history one enemy changes sides: `Cleric of Innoruuk`, which took
22,633 with 2,803 of healing along the way — without subtracting it, it crossed
the threshold and came out labelled a raid boss.

## And two slows that were not being announced

`Languid Pace` and `Instill` fell into no category, so "Root and slow" did not
warn about them even with the switch on. They are in now, measured: only the
ones cast by an enemy are announced — 44 % of them were cast by your own pet —
and that comes to two per fight in 9 % of them.

Three other groups of uncategorised spells were looked at — lifetaps, stuns and
mana drains — and **none of them gets announced**: one every eight seconds, or
one every two, is not an alert. A voice that talks constantly teaches you not to
listen to it.
