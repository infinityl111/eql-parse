## The update notice no longer offers what does not exist

This version fixes one thing, and it is the one that happened to you if you
opened the app on the afternoon of 17 August.

**What was happening.** 1.16.0 was published as the newest version with its
notes uploaded and **without the installer**. The notice appeared in
full: "there is a new version", the notes translated into your language, and a
button reading **Download** that led to a page with nothing to download.

**What changes.** If a version is published and carries no installer, **no
notice is shown at all**. No button, no notes, no promise. And nothing showing
up does not mean the check failed: the version is half published, and the notice
will appear on its own once it is finished. There is nothing to do on your
machine.

**And no skip button.** That is the part that could not be undone. "Skip this
version" is written to your settings, so anyone who got tired of the broken
notice and skipped it **would have missed the good version** once the installer
finally went up. Now there is no notice, so there is no button to press: it
follows from the shape of the code rather than from remembering to remove it.

## What stays the same

If you can update, you are still told. There is a case that looks like this one
and is not: when the installer is there but has to be downloaded from the page
instead of from the program. That one works, and it still tells you as always.

> "Cannot be installed from here" and "there is no installer" are not the same
> thing, and until now they were written the same way.

**Not one figure changes.** It does not touch your history, does not ask you to
rebuild anything, and does not change how a single fight is measured. It opens
just like 1.16.0 and with the same data.
